# HeadBlade Germany — Owner Review Checklist

Use the public review URL only after all automated release gates are green.

Review these points on desktop and mobile:

- Homepage: Contour Line, “Für deinen Kopf gebaut.” hierarchy, MOTO/ATX choice, compatibility, routine, manual-vs-electric decision help, and support links
- Navigation: Rasierer, Klingen, Pflege, Sets, Finder, So geht’s, Vergleichen; native mobile menu must be keyboard-operable
- HeadBlade Fit: answers stay local in the browser; recommendation and reasons update without a form or profile
- Comparison routes: MOTO vs ATX, HB4 vs HB6, manual vs electric; tables must remain readable and scrollable on narrow screens
- Authority guides: Kopf richtig rasieren, Rasurbrand vermeiden, Welche Klingen passen?
- Product detail pages, especially MOTO and ATX: media gallery, “Passt zu dir, wenn …”, compatibility, application guidance, and nearest alternative
- Product names, prices shown for owner review, compatibility wording, claims, bundle descriptions, and contact/company details
- No purchase, checkout, payment, order, merchant `Offer` schema, or customer form can be completed or emitted in review mode
- No visible broken links, missing images, console errors, unintended horizontal page overflow, or major layout shifts
- Review banner and robots controls clearly identify the non-production state
- Reduced-motion preference must not animate/transition the Contour signature line

Approval of the visual owner review does **not** deploy production automatically. Production requires a separate explicit owner instruction and the dedicated production gate.
